UNVEIL
Discipline over Motivation — a habit game that unveils a picture

Hang one picture you want to finish, then sign a contract: a duration and your daily pledges.
Each day you keep your word, a piece of the cover falls away.
Complete the journey, and the picture is yours.

Why

Motivation is fickle; discipline stays. Instead of cheers and badges, Unveil offers a contract with yourself. Today's pledges lock the moment you register them, and nothing changes them until the day ends. There is no punishment for a failed day — no pieces are revealed and your finish date slips by one, but the picture always waits for you.

How it works

1Hang a picture — your own photo, or a built-in artwork chosen by keywords only (a mystery until unveiled)
2Sign the contract — duration (7–365 days), daily pledges, reset hour, reveal mode
3Each morning — register and lock today's pledges (or let auto-renew do it for you)
4Through the day — check off pledges; the last check makes pieces cascade away
5Finish — write a closing note, then watch the journey: the picture unveils again while every day's pledges scroll beneath it

Choose how tight the contract is

Standard
A failed day reveals nothing and pushes the finish date back by one. The picture is always preserved — the deadline simply moves.
Relaxed
No failure at all. Each day opens as much as you actually completed. For habits you want to build without a verdict hanging over you.
Deadline
The goal ends on the day you set, no matter what. Finish 80% or more and it closes as a partial win — the picture is archived exactly as far as you got, with its own frame. An honest record beats a fake trophy.

Reveal & pledge modes

  • Classic — one pledge = one piece, with a finale on the last day
  • Cinematic — each successful day unveils 1/duration of hundreds of pieces
  • Fixed / decide daily / auto-renew — keep the same pledges, choose the count each morning, or let a locked set carry over as a standing contract (edits apply from tomorrow)
  • Growing numbers — tap a number in a pledge and it grows by +N each completed day (100 push-ups → 101 → 102…). Failed days never raise the bar; hitting your target locks it in with a celebration

In practice

A 100-day push-up challenge
Pledge “100 push-ups”, set auto-increase +1 and a target of 200. Every completed day raises the bar by one — and the picture with it.
Studying for an exam, with a hard deadline
Deadline mode until the exam date, cinematic reveal, and a daily word count you choose each morning. Even at 84% you finish with a partial win to keep.
Fixed routines — reading, workouts, meditation
Auto-renew turns your locked pledges into a standing contract that carries over daily, even if you never open the app.

What you get back

  • Patterns — golden hours (when you actually finish things), weekday patterns, per-pledge success rates, and a shareable monthly report card. All free, all computed on your device
  • Activity calendar — a year of discipline at a glance
  • Logbook — every day's pledges, verdict and one-line note
  • Gallery — finished pictures framed, with a timelapse replay of the whole journey
  • Gifts — wrap a picture into a file and send it through any messenger. The receiver unveils it without ever seeing what it is, and it stays locked in their library until they finish a goal with it
  • Your palette — four accent colors × four dark backgrounds

Principles

  • Honest verdicts — PERFECT / PARTIAL (exactly one short, never twice in a row) / FAILED / SKIPPED
  • Delay, not punishment — a failed day reveals nothing; the finish date slips, the picture is preserved
  • Locked means locked — once you register today's pledges, they cannot be edited or deleted until the day ends
  • Local-first — no account, no server, no ads, no tracking. The app does not even use the internet permission
  • Quiet notifications — they fire only when something is unfinished; a completed day stays silent

Generation 2 — being built now

Everything above is the finished first generation. What follows is in development, and grew out of one question: what makes an app worth opening, rather than one more thing to keep up with?

One screen for today
However many goals you are running, everything you promised today sits on the hub, ready to check off. Locking a contract still happens inside each goal — that is where the picture is, and the promise belongs next to it.
The name is the progress bar
UNVEIL is drawn out of 61 pieces. Today's completions fill it with amber, and the last one makes the whole word flash. The idea of the app, in its own name.
An opening worth seeing once a day
The cover peels away in a diagonal wave and the name is underneath. Once a day, on your first visit — never a toll for someone who came to check one box.
Start tomorrow
Set a goal up tonight and let the clock start in the morning.
